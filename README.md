## Helium Management


The Helium Management (HM) project is concerned with developing software and hardware for the management of helium for research facilities. The project was started by the Helmholtz-Zentrum Berlin (HZB) and further developed within a collaboration of the HZB, the Institut Laue Langevin (ILL), the ISIS Neutron and Muon Source, the European Spallation Source (ESS) and the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) under the umbrella of the International Society for Sample Environment (ISSE).

Since 2026 the HM hardware and software are commercially available via the company [HZDRI](https://hzdr-innovation.de/en/hzdr-innovation-produkte/helium-management-system/). 

For a detailed documentation please refer to the [wiki](https://github.com/SampleEnvironment/Helium-Management/wiki)
